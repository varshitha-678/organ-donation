Understand organ donation

Use trusted, official sources to learn about donation and the process in your region.

What is organ donation?

Organ donation is the process by which a person may donate eligible organs, according to applicable laws, medical criteria and consent procedures, to help people who need transplants. The process differs by country and circumstances.

Important: This website provides general educational information only. It does not determine medical eligibility, register you with a government authority, or replace legal consent procedures.

Common questions

Can anyone automatically become a donor by using this website?

No. Submitting this project's form only records an expression of interest in this educational prototype. It is not official registration or legally valid consent.

Should I discuss my wishes with family?

It is sensible to talk openly with family or trusted people and learn the official rules and procedures that apply in your location.

Does this website decide who can donate?

No. Medical suitability and donation decisions are handled by qualified professionals and authorized organizations under applicable rules.

Is organ donation the same as blood donation?

No. They are different processes with different requirements, procedures and official guidance.

How to start a conversation

  • Read information from official health authorities.
  • Write down questions and discuss them with your family.
  • Check local eligibility, consent and registration requirements.
  • Keep your decision voluntary and informed.
